# JobDue Brand System

JobDue is a practical no-login browser tracker for solo service pros who need to see job status, payment status, and follow-up work before the day starts.

- Product name: JobDue
- Tagline: Track jobs. Follow up. Get paid.
- Contact email: hi.craniai@gmail.com
- Staging URL: https://app.hi-craniai.workers.dev
- Purchase URL placeholder: https://gumroad.com/l/jobdue-launch-placeholder

## Positioning

JobDue helps solo contractors track jobs, balances, notes, and follow-ups in one local browser app. It is not full accounting software, dispatch software, automatic SMS, online payment processing, or cloud sync.

## Voice

- Direct, calm, and practical.
- Say what the buyer can do today.
- Prefer simple words: jobs, balances, follow-ups, backup, browser, no monthly bill.
- Avoid unsupported claims, hype, fake testimonials, and full CRM comparisons.
