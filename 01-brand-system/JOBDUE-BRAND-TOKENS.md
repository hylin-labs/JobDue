# JobDue Brand Tokens

## Color

- Page background: `#EEF3F5`
- Soft background: `#F7FAFB`
- Card background: `#FFFFFF`
- Main text: `#162326`
- Muted text: `#5D6B70`
- Primary teal: `#247C82`
- Primary dark: `#155A60`
- Primary soft: `#DCEEEF`
- Money accent: `#D97706`
- Success: `#168A5B`
- Danger: `#C2412D`
- Border: `#D8E2E5`

## Typography

- Font stack: `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`
- Hero H1: large, heavy, tight line height
- Section H2: clear, direct, less heavy than hero
- Card H3: short benefit title
- Body: 16-18px with relaxed line height
- Eyebrows: uppercase with teal accent bar

## UI Style

- White working cards on Cool Mist background
- Steel Teal primary buttons
- Soft borders and restrained shadows
- Orange only for money or overdue emphasis
- Screenshot-led proof, not stock imagery
