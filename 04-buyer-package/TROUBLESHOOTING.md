# Troubleshooting

## JobDue Opens As Text

Right-click `JobDue.html`, choose **Open with**, then choose Chrome, Edge, or Safari.

If you are on a phone or tablet, use the hosted JobDue install link if one was included with your purchase. Local HTML files can be harder to open, find, and pin on phones and tablets.

## I Want To Use JobDue On iPhone Or iPad

Best iPhone and iPad path:

1. Open `https://app.hi-craniai.workers.dev` in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Open JobDue from the new home screen icon.

The included `iPhone-iPad-Setup-Video.mp4` video shows these steps visually.

Your jobs are saved in Safari on that iPhone or iPad. Use **Export Backup** to protect or move your data.

## I Do Not See Add To Home Screen On iPhone Or iPad

Make sure the JobDue install link is open in **Safari**.

If the link opened inside Gmail, Facebook, Messages, Etsy, Gumroad, or another app, tap the browser/share option and open the page in Safari first.

Then tap **Share** and look for **Add to Home Screen**. You may need to scroll through the Share menu.

## I Want To Use JobDue On Android Phone Or Tablet

Best Android path:

1. Open `https://app.hi-craniai.workers.dev` in **Chrome**.
2. If Chrome shows an install message, tap **Install app**.
3. If you do not see that message, tap the Chrome menu.
4. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
5. Open JobDue from the new home screen icon.

The included `Android-Setup-Video.mp4` video shows these steps visually.

Your jobs are saved in Chrome on that Android device. Use **Export Backup** to protect or move your data.

## I Do Not See Install App On Android

Make sure the JobDue install link is open in **Chrome**.

If the link opened inside Gmail, Facebook, Messages, Etsy, Gumroad, or another app, open the page in Chrome first.

Then tap the Chrome menu and look for **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

If the install option still does not appear, you can still use JobDue in Chrome. Keep the page bookmarked and use **Export Backup** often.

## I Installed JobDue On iPhone, iPad, Or Android But My Jobs Are Missing

JobDue does not automatically sync across devices.

Jobs entered on a computer do not automatically appear on a phone or tablet. Export a `.json` backup from the old device, move it to the new device, then use **Import Backup**.

## My Phone And Tablet Show Different Jobs

Each device has its own saved browser data.

Use **Export Backup** on the device that has the correct jobs, then use **Import Backup** on the other device.

## The Hosted Link Changed And My Jobs Are Missing

If the hosted URL changes, your phone or tablet may treat it like a different app.

Open the old link if you still have access to it and export a backup. Then open the new link and import that `.json` backup.

## I Cannot Find My Backup File

Check your browser's Downloads area first.

On iPhone and iPad, also check the Files app, iCloud Drive, Downloads folder, or the folder you selected when saving.

On Android, also check the Files app, Downloads folder, Google Drive, or the folder you selected when saving.

Search for a file name like:

`contractor-tracker-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`

## I Cannot Find A Backup File On My Phone Or Tablet

Check the browser download list first.

On iPhone and iPad, open the Files app and check Downloads, iCloud Drive, and Recent.

On Android, open the Files app and check Downloads, Google Drive, and Recent.

If you saved the backup as an email attachment or cloud-drive file, open that app and search for `contractor-tracker-backup`.

## My Jobs Are Missing After Switching Browsers

JobDue saves jobs in the browser where you entered them.

For example, jobs saved in Chrome do not automatically appear in Edge or Safari.

Open the original browser and use **Data > Export Backup**, then import that backup in the new browser.

## My Jobs Are Missing After Switching Devices

JobDue does not automatically sync across devices.

Use **Export Backup** on the old device, move the `.json` backup file to the new device, then use **Import Backup** on the new device.

## I Cleared Browser Data And My Jobs Disappeared

Clearing browser data can erase jobs saved by JobDue.

If you have a `.json` backup, open JobDue and use **Import Backup**.

If you do not have a backup, the erased browser data may not be recoverable.

## Import Backup Says The File Is Invalid

Make sure you selected a JobDue `.json` backup file.

CSV files cannot restore JobDue.

If the backup still fails, keep your current data unchanged and try another backup file.

## I Opened JobDue In Private Or Incognito Mode

Private or incognito windows are not recommended for real work.

Open JobDue in a normal Chrome, Edge, or Safari window, then export a backup after adding jobs.

## I Only Have The Local JobDue.html File On My Phone

Local `JobDue.html` files can open as previews or be hard to find again on some phones and tablets.

For iPhone and iPad, use the hosted JobDue install link in Safari when available. Then tap **Share > Add to Home Screen**.

For Android, use the hosted JobDue install link in Chrome when available. Then tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

If you must use the local file, save it in the Files app and open it with Safari or Chrome, then export backups often.

## I Expected Cloud Sync

JobDue V1 does not include automatic cloud sync.

Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, email, and Downloads can store backup files, but they do not sync live app data.

## I Expected Invoice Sending Or Payments

JobDue tracks who needs an invoice, who has paid, and who needs follow-up.

It does not send invoices, process payments, connect to QuickBooks, or manage team dispatch.

## Still Need Help?

Contact support through the store where you purchased JobDue.

Please include:

- Your device: Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, or Chromebook
- Your browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari, or another browser
- What you clicked before the problem happened
- A screenshot if possible

Do not send your backup file unless support asks for it.
