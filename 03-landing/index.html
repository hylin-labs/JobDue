Buyer questions

Know exactly what you are buying.

Is this cloud software?

No. JobDue does not use an account or cloud database. Your records are saved in the browser/device where you use the app.

Will my phone and computer sync automatically?

No. Records do not automatically sync between devices or browsers. To move records, export a backup and import it on the other device or browser.

Is this an App Store or Play Store app?

No. JobDue is a browser-based tracker. The buyer package includes setup videos for opening and saving it on iPhone, iPad, and Android browsers.

Does JobDue send invoices or collect payments?

No. JobDue tracks invoice/payment status and follow-up needs. It does not send invoices, process payments, or connect to accounting software.

Does it replace accounting software?

No. JobDue is for job tracking, payment status, and follow-up visibility. Accounting and tax records should stay in your accounting system.

How do I protect my data?

Use Export Backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe. Import Backup can restore or move your records.

Who is JobDue best for?

JobDue is best for solo handymen, cleaners, landscapers, painters, and home-service pros who want a simple tracker without full contractor software.

Who is it not for?

It is not for dispatch teams, multi-user crews, businesses that need GPS routing, QuickBooks integration, automatic SMS, invoice sending, or online payment processing.
